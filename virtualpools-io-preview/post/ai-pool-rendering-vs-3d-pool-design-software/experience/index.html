AI generates a picture. VirtualPools runs the sales conversation.

Published: June 15, 2026

If you only need a nice image, use ChatGPT. VirtualPools is for the moment your customer says “make it a metre longer”. A picture cannot do that. A configurable 3D model of your own products can, live, in the meeting, and that is what turns a design conversation into a signed pool.

Tuesday evening, the customer’s kitchen table. The builder opens the laptop. On the screen: a photorealistic image of an eight by four metre swimming pool in this exact garden, made yesterday from the CAD drawing with a good prompt. The customer leans in. “That is beautiful.”

Four changes. Four normal changes. The customer is not being difficult; the customer is buying. This is the moment the sale is decided, and it is decided by what the builder can do in the next sixty seconds.

The customer asks for four changes: one metre longer, stairs on the left, a darker liner, the terrace fifty centimetres wider.

The same meeting

A picture has no state. An AI image generator does not know what a pool is; it knows what pool pictures look like. When you prompt it again, it does not open yesterday’s pool and edit it; it produces a new picture that resembles the description. A configurator works the other way round: VirtualPools holds one 3D model of your pool, and a change is an edit to that model. Only what you change changes.

Our CAD software has a render button too.

CAD stays where it belongs, in the technical drawing after the sale. It does, and it renders the technical model correctly. Two things are missing at the kitchen table: speed and the customer. A CAD render takes minutes and a trained drafter; the customer cannot touch it. VirtualPools is built for the conversation: the builder or the customer changes the pool in seconds, on a tablet, and the visual and the AR preview update from the same model.

My drafter does the updates in the evening.

The drafter is not the problem; the delay between question and answer is. Then every revision round costs one evening of drafter time and one day of customer waiting. Ten revision rounds a month is ten evenings and ten days of open decisions. Move the revision into the meeting and the drafter draws the pools that were actually sold.

ChatGPT will be able to edit images next year.

Image editing is improving fast, and for a mood image that is welcome. An edited picture still does not know your catalogue, your dimensions, your price list or the customer's slope. VirtualPools is not competing on picture quality; it is competing on being the pool itself, configurable, in the customer's garden. Better pictures make the first ten minutes of the meeting easier. They do not close it.

My customers just want a nice picture.

A nice picture is where every conversation starts. They want a nice picture of their pool, and they only find out it is theirs by changing it. Customers start playing. They move the stairs themselves. They try three liners. Playing is deciding. A static picture is admired; a configurable pool is chosen.

Does ChatGPT modify the existing pool model, or does it generate another image?

It generates another image. An image generator has no model to modify: every prompt produces a new interpretation of the whole picture, so the liner, the stairs and the terrace can all shift even when you only asked for one change. VirtualPools edits one configurable 3D model, so a change request changes only the parameter you touched.

Which AI tool is best for pool design?

For an inspiration image, ChatGPT, Gemini, Midjourney and the dedicated AI pool-visualiser apps all do a good job, and they are cheap. For the sales meeting, where the customer asks for changes and expects to see the same pool with only those changes, none of them: that job needs a configurable 3D model of the products you actually sell.

Can you render a pool in a photo of the customer's garden?

Yes. VirtualPools places your pool design in the customer's garden two ways: on a satellite image of the plot, and in true-to-scale augmented reality through a tablet camera. Hill Mode adjusts the pool to sloped terrain.

Can I use my own product catalogue?

Yes. Your configurator is built with your pool shapes, dimensions, stairs, liners, mosaics, coping stones and lights, under your own branding. Catalogues from manufacturers such as Renolit, Compass Pools, Duratech, Fluvo, LPW Pools, Haogenplast, T&A and Starline are already available in VirtualPools.

Is there a free app for pool design?

Yes, several free consumer apps and AI image tools exist, and they are fine for a homeowner's first idea. VirtualPools is built for the pool builder who sells in meetings: your catalogue, your branding, your customer's garden, live changes. That is a different job.

Does VirtualPools also produce the quote?

Today VirtualPools produces the design, the visualisation and the AR preview; the quote follows from the configured design in your own process. Automatic price estimation from the design is on the VirtualPools roadmap for November 2026.

We already have a designer who does the revisions. Why change?

Keep the designer. The point is where the revision happens: after the meeting, by email, or in the meeting, with the customer watching. A revision that arrives two days later restarts the decision; a revision the customer sees live closes it.

How much does 3D pool rendering cost with VirtualPools?

VirtualPools is a monthly subscription per pool builder, not a price per render or per project. Renders, changes and AR sessions are unlimited within the subscription. Ask for current pricing in a founder demo; there is no sales team in between.

Is VirtualPools 3D pool design software or a rendering tool?

Both, in one model: the configurator is 3D pool design software built on your own catalogue, and every view of the configured pool is a rendering of that same model. That is why a change request edits the pool instead of generating another picture.
