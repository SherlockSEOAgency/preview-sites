For pool builders who already use CAD + ChatGPT

AI generates a picture. VirtualPools runs the sales conversation.

If you only need a nice image, use ChatGPT. VirtualPools is for the moment your customer says “make it a metre longer”.

“Beautiful. Now make the pool one metre longer, stairs on the left, a darker liner, and the terrace 50 cm wider.”

bron: herordend

Every pool builder hears this sentence in every sales meeting. It is the moment the sale is won or lost. Not when the first image appears, but when the customer asks for the second one.

If you already use CAD and ChatGPT, you can make a convincing first image for a few euros. This page is about what happens after that sentence.

What happens next with CAD + ChatGPT

Back to CAD and adjust the model Export the design again Upload it and write a new prompt Regenerate the image Get a new picture that is a new interpretation: the liner is close, the stairs moved, the terrace changed shape Customer: “That is not the same pool.” The momentum in the room is gone.

What happens next in VirtualPools

bron: klant-copy

Length: +1 m Stairs: left Liner: darker, from your own catalogue Terrace: +50 cm The same pool changes in front of the customer. Nothing else moves. Customer: “Yes, that one.”

A picture has no state. A model does.

bron: herordend

ChatGPT does not know that this must stay exactly the same commercial pool with four controlled changes. It generates a new image, and every generation is a fresh guess.

VirtualPools keeps one configurable 3D model of your products alive through the whole conversation. Only what you change changes. That is the difference between a rendering tool and a sales tool, and it is why VirtualPools does not replace your AI images. It replaces the back-and-forth.

Questions pool builders ask about AI and 3D pool design

Does ChatGPT modify the existing pool model, or does it generate another image?
It generates another image. An image generator has no model to modify; each prompt produces a new interpretation. VirtualPools edits one configurable 3D model, so a change request changes only what you changed.
Which AI tool is best for pool design?
For an inspiration image, any of the well-known image generators will do. For the sales meeting, where the customer asks for changes and expects to see the same pool, none of them: that needs a configurable model of your own products.
Can you render a pool from a photo of the garden?
Yes. VirtualPools places your pool design in the customer's garden using satellite imagery and true-to-scale augmented reality on a tablet, including sloped terrain.
Can I use my own product catalogue?
Yes. Your configurator is built with your pool shapes, dimensions, stairs, liners, mosaics, coping stones and lights, under your own branding. Catalogues from leading pool manufacturers are already available.
How much does 3D rendering cost with VirtualPools?
VirtualPools is a monthly subscription per builder, not a price per render. Ask for the current pricing in a founder demo; there is no sales team in between.
Is there a free app for pool design?
Free consumer apps and AI image tools exist and are fine for a first idea. VirtualPools is built for pool builders who sell in meetings, which is a different job.
